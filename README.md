<details>
<summary><strong>🔐 Will you log into my account?</strong></summary>
<br>

**No.** We only need your public Steam ID (or profile URL). We never access your account, and we never ask for your password or Steam Guard code. All commend operations go through our 2000+ bot accounts.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>⚠️ Is there a VAC ban risk?</strong></summary>
<br>

**No.** Our system never touches your account — our bots use Steam's official commend feature on you. VAC detects cheating; commending is a legitimate gameplay feature provided by Valve itself.
</details>

Commends are sent **within seconds** by our bots. Steam's profile counter updates within **1–24 hours** (Valve's own sync delay). Trust Factor changes may take **a few days** to reflect.
